Behind the counter

A day at Bakerz Bite

From the first tray to the last box, here is how a day in our bakery rolls along.

  1. 6:00 AM

    Ovens on

    Dough is shaped and the first trays slide in.

  2. 9:00 AM

    Doors open

    Warm croissants and morning pastries meet the counter.

  3. 12:30 PM

    Cake o'clock

    Celebration cakes are layered, frosted and finished by hand.

  4. 4:00 PM

    Tea-time batch

    Fresh cookies and pies for the golden-hour rush.

  5. 10:00 PM

    Last box

    The final order is packed and the ovens finally rest.
